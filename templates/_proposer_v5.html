{# Paper book: Sensex V5 - Sensex V4 with a 150-point SENSEX loss floor instead of the -20% premium floor #}

Sensex V5 (paper)

Sensex V4 — the live preset proposer_dt35_v3 (SENSEX Proposer v3 rules, a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_v5_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.035) * 100) }}% day target, the with-the-day filter, Sentinel's |delta| ≥ 0.60 strike, a 270 s wait after an exit, {{ proposer_v5_stats.lots or 100 }} lots, from 1 July) — with one change, the 150 rule: no premium floor; a trade is closed once SENSEX is 150 points against its entry. Exits marked spot_floor are that rule.

Backtest, 1 Jul – 16 Oct, 100 lots, after charges, on the collector's spot bars: +₹10.75L (max drawdown ₹2.82L, worst day −₹2.36L) against V4's +₹8.16L (₹4.65L, −₹2.56L); halves Jul–Aug +₹3.79L, Sep–Oct +₹6.97L. Four trades hit the floor, each losing ₹1.88–2.36L. Caveats: 150 was picked in-sample — one of four values (80, 100, 150, 200) tried on the same data; it has not been combined with the expiry-day skip; there is no SENSEX tick data, so the 150-point trigger runs on the same simulated in-minute path (one-minute bars and recorded snapshots) as every other exit, and a real stop could fill worse on a fast move. Sessions after 16 Oct are the first the rule was not chosen on. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.
