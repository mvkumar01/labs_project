Crypto taker imbalance (paper)

Binance USDT perpetual futures. Each day at 00:00 UTC, take the {{ st.top or 50 }} most liquid contracts and rank them by who was the aggressor over the last 24 hours: (taker buys − taker sells) / volume. Go long the most bought and short the most sold, in equal dollars (rank-weighted, market-neutral, gross exposure = equity). The book held is the average of the last {{ st.hold_days or 7 }} daily targets, traded at the 01:00 UTC price. Result = price moves − funding paid − {{ "%.0f"|format(st.cost_bps or 7) }} bps of everything traded. Paper equity starts at ${{ "{:,.0f}".format(st.equity_start or 100000) }} on {{ st.paper_start or '2026-06-01' }}.

Evidence status: a candidate being watched, not a proven edge. The rule comes from the crypto research project and is fixed. Two periods are kept apart and must not be pooled: 1 June – 31 August 2026 is covered by the research backtest (where it lost 6.6% to 30 August, its last complete day), and this book has to reproduce it; from {{ st.first_unseen or '2026-09-01' }} the research had not seen the data, and no parameter may be tuned on it. Limits of this ledger: the server cannot reach Binance's live API, so it reads Binance's public archive — the book runs about two days behind; funding for the current month is estimated from the premium index until Binance publishes the month's rates, then replaced; and whether a contract could be traded at the rebalance is judged from its own bar (a price and volume in the hour to 01:00), not from the exchange's status. Fills are assumed at the hourly close with a flat cost; order-book depth in the smaller contracts is not modelled.
