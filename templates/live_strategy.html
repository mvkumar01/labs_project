Timing is the recorded v2.11 champion ledger (same entries, exits, and sides as the Alpha v2.11 tab) — only the instrument changes. Strikes are set from each trade's entry spot: ATM = nearest 50, ITM/OTM = ±200 points. Rationale: on a breakeven spot stop a naked long option still loses (theta / IV / spread), while a delta≈1 synthetic books ≈0 by construction and a debit spread hard-caps the loss. The plain synthetic carries an UNCAPPED short leg (and its margin); the three-leg protected synthetic buys an OTM200 hedge against that short leg, capping the structural tail risk at roughly the 200-point gap for the extra hedge debit. SELL legs are priced bid-in / ask-out. Charges are applied per leg, so multi-leg baskets pay a multiple of a single option's charges.
