Risk core · trend × vol (paper)

Daily. ₹10,00,000 notional in five equal sleeves of ₹2,00,000 — NIFTY 50, BANK NIFTY, BTC, ETH and MCX crude — set back to equal on the first day of each month. In each sleeve, at every close, the share held long for the next day is trend × min(1, 15% / volatility): trend is the average of nine 0/1 rules (close above its 20 / 50 / 100 / 200-day average; Donchian 20 and 55 breakouts; close above its level 30 / 90 / 180 days ago), volatility the RiskMetrics estimate (λ 0.94), annualised. The share changes only when the target moves by 0.10 or more, or falls to zero. Idle money earns nothing. Costs a side: index 0.14%, crypto 0.07% plus Binance funding, crude 0.05% plus 0.1% a month roll; crypto in rupees at the daily USD/INR rate.

A protection layer, not a profit engine (V2 Strategy Tester). In research it cut the worst fall of holding the five markets from −37% to −5%, but over the last two years it earned below cash. The research figures (CAGR +11.6%, Sharpe 0.89 above 6% cash, worst fall −5.3%) also credited 6% interest on idle index money and crude collateral and index dividends; with idle money earning nothing, as here, the same years give about +9%, Sharpe 0.47, worst fall −6.3%. Research period to 15 October 2026, forward from 16 October. Stated rule: the protection is broken if the forward worst fall goes beyond −15%, about three times the research worst fall.
