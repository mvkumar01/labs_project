GOLD CCI short (paper)

Strategy Tester v2, MCX gold: short when the 5-minute CCI(14) turns above 100 while the 15-minute RSI(5) is above 50 and the 1-hour DI− is above DI+ (ADX length 7). Entry at the next 1-minute bar's open, one position at a time, 30-bar cooldown between signals. Stop 0.25% above the entry; once a completed bar has traded one stop distance in favour the stop moves to the entry price; target three stop distances; flat at 23:29. {{ gold_cci_stats.lots or 1 }} lot of GOLD (1 kg, ₹{{ gold_cci_stats.qty or 100 }} per rupee of price).

Evidence status: a fit, not a tested edge. The rule was found on GOLD26OCTFUT candles from 10 March to 25 September 2026 (found again from five different sets of training weeks, and it held on each run's own unseen weeks). The book is filled back to {{ gold_cci_stats.paper_start or '2026-06-01' }}, but only sessions from {{ gold_cci_stats.first_unseen or '2026-09-28' }} are ones the rule had not seen; the two stretches are shown apart below. To 25 September the book uses the October contract the Tester used; from 28 September the December contract. Costs: MCX futures charges plus one tick (₹1) of slippage each side, about ₹2,850 a round trip. One full stop costs about ₹40,000 on one lot.
