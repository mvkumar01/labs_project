Nifty Scalper V4 2.5% · fixed clock (paper)

Nifty Scalper V4 at 2.5% on a fixed print clock with a 2-loss stop — the controllable set-up recommended after the 9 Nov 2026 live/paper audit. Two changes from the Nifty Scalper V4 2.5% book: every print is acted on exactly 30 s into its minute (prints every 3 minutes from 09:22, always on complete SENSEX data; the live runner's print second drifts through the minute and about one print in six is built before that minute's SENSEX bar is written), and no new entry after the day's 2nd losing trade. Everything else is Nifty Scalper V4 2.5%: the day banks once today's realized profit plus the open trade reaches {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_nifty_v4_dt25_fc_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.025) * 100) }}% of the trade's entry capital, whatever the book; out early once NIFTY is 35 points against within 10 minutes; CPR-room and reversal-candle entry blocks; NIFTY options (|delta| ≥ 0.60, ₹12L of premium per entry), scaled spot targets and floor, a 270 s wait after an exit. One-minute simulator to 24 Oct, recorded ticks from 27 Oct. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.

Research, 1 Jul – 9 Nov 2026 (alphaIMB det_test.py, fixed_report.py), stop after the 2nd loss, at a fixed print second: 1.5% +₹6.91L at 30 s (worst day −₹2.01L; +₹1.73L..+₹6.91L at 10–45 s), 2.5% +₹10.05L at 30 s (worst day −₹3.40L; −₹1.52L..+₹11.78L at 10–45 s). With the stop the worst day stayed at −₹2.0L..−₹3.8L (about two floor exits, 17–32% of the first trade's premium) at every second; without it up to −₹8.0L. Caveats: in-sample; the V4 rules were tuned at 25 s and before 27 Oct the second decides where a trade enters on a made-up one-minute path, so the totals swing with the second; on the 10 recorded tick days the stop kept both books positive at every second. The loss count is the engine's (gross below zero). Companion: Nifty Scalper V4 1.5% · fixed clock.
