Sensex 2.5% V6 · IV half (paper)

Sensex 2.5% V6 — Sensex V5 2.5% — the live preset proposer_dt35_v5 rules (SENSEX Proposer v3, the with-the-day filter, Sentinel's |delta| ≥ 0.60 strike, a 270 s wait after an exit, no premium floor, a trade closed once SENSEX is 150 points against its entry, {{ proposer_v6_dt25_ivh_stats.lots or 100 }} lots, a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_v6_dt25_ivh_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.025) * 100) }}% day target) — with one change: no entry while SENSEX is inside the previous session's CPR (between TC and BC, ends included). A held signal waits for the next print; entries above or below CPR, exits and everything else are unchanged — with one change, the size: at 09:30 the SENSEX ATM implied vol of the traded weekly (the average of the ATM call's and put's IV, each from its bid/ask mid) is compared with the median 09:30 IV of the earlier sessions from {{ (proposer_v6_dt25_ivh_stats.iv_half or {}).since or '2026-07-01' }}. Above {{ (proposer_v6_dt25_ivh_stats.iv_half or {}).ratio or 1.2 }}× that median (once {{ (proposer_v6_dt25_ivh_stats.iv_half or {}).min_sessions or 10 }} sessions are in it), the whole day trades 1,000 qty (50 lots) instead of 2,000; the day target stays 2.5% of the open position's premium, so it halves too. A missing IV means full size, and so does an entry before 09:30 (the IV is not known yet).

Research, 1 Jul – 6 Nov 2026 (one-minute replay on bid/ask mid to 24 Oct, recorded ticks from 27 Oct, book carried): +₹12.07L, worst day −₹1.52L, max drawdown ₹2.34L, against V6 at full size in the same run +₹11.87L, −₹3.04L, ₹4.68L; 25 half-size days (alphaIMB sizing_test.py iv_hard1.2). Caveats: the high-IV weakness was found on this same data, and most of the gain is two days — 26 Oct (−₹3.04L → −₹1.52L) and 27 Oct (−₹1.64L → −₹0.82L) — so the sessions from 9 Nov are the test. This book prices sessions before 27 Oct on LTP, not the mid, so its totals differ from the research's. Before 27 Oct there is no SENSEX tick data, so exits there run on one-minute bars and recorded snapshots; from 27 Oct the sessions are replayed on the recorded SENSEX ticks (Replay column). Paper only: no live preset, it never calls a broker order API.
