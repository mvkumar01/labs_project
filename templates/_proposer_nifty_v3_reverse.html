{# Paper book: Nifty Scalper V3 Reverse #}

Nifty Scalper V3 Reverse (paper)

Nifty Scalper V3's trades, held in the opposite option — the paper copy of the live preset Nifty Scalper V3 Reverse. The V3 trade runs unseen as a shadow: the predictor printing every 3 minutes, the Sensex V5 entry gate with the 75-point SENSEX with-the-day filter, its NIFTY contract, scaled spot targets and 150-point floor, a 270 s wait after an exit and the {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_nifty_v3_reverse_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.015) * 100) }}% day target and recovery book on the shadow's own P&L. When the shadow buys a CALL this book buys a PUT (and the reverse): the same picker on the other side (next weekly on NIFTY's expiry day, |delta| ≥ 0.60), ₹12L of premium, bought at the ask as the shadow enters and sold at the bid as the shadow exits, whatever the reason.

The live preset sizes the held option by the account's configured lots; this book uses ₹12L of premium per entry like the other Nifty Scalper books. The shadow is simulated exactly as the Nifty Scalper V3 book, so the V3 shadow column should match that tab's gross. Caveats: before 27 Oct the one-minute simulator (no tick data), which misses fills decided within seconds; from 27 Oct replayed on the recorded ticks (Replay column). Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.
