NIFTY Tuesday put (paper)

On every Tuesday that is a trading day, one lot ({{ st.qty or 65 }} units) of the NIFTY put of the nearest expiry is bought at 09:20 and sold at 15:25 — since 2 September 2025 that is the contract expiring that day. Strike = round((NIFTY 09:20 minute open + 89) / 50) × 50, about 89 points in the money. No stop, no target. A holiday Tuesday is skipped, never moved to another day.

Evidence status: a weak, recent lead, not a tested edge. An edge-birth scan in the Strategy Tester found the index falling from 09:20 to 15:25 on Tuesdays (−22.5 bps in 2024, −13.2 in 2025, −13.1 in 2026; flat to positive in 2020–22); priced on option quotes, the put made about +₹1,478 a lot a day on 42 Tuesdays (t +1.4). Tuesdays up to {{ st.sample_end or '2026-09-01' }} are the research sample; later ones are the forward test. The rules are fixed and are not tuned from what this book shows. Retirement, stated in advance: if the first 26 forward Tuesdays net to zero or less after charges, the book is marked “retire”. Headline fills: bought at the ask, sold at the bid, then the standard option charges; a side with no quote uses the research price worsened by 0.2 points. Tuesdays before the shared store (to 21 April 2026) have only five-minute last traded prices, so their headline is that price ± 0.2. The research version is the research's own figure: the middle of bid and ask when quoted and tight, else the last traded price, minus a flat ₹95.
