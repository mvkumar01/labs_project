Sensex V5 2.5% (paper)

Sensex V5 — the live preset proposer_dt35_v5 (SENSEX Proposer v3 rules, the with-the-day filter, Sentinel's |delta| ≥ 0.60 strike, a 270 s wait after an exit, no premium floor and a trade closed once SENSEX is 150 points against its entry, {{ proposer_v5_dt25_stats.lots or 100 }} lots, from 1 July) — with one change: a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_v5_dt25_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.025) * 100) }}% day target instead of 3.5%, so a day ends once realized profit plus the open trade reaches 2.5% of the trade's entry capital.

Backtest, 1 Jul – 16 Oct, 100 lots, after charges, on the collector's spot bars: +₹9.76L (max drawdown ₹1.97L, worst day −₹1.97L, 3 red days, 78 trades) against V5 at 3.5% +₹10.75L (₹2.82L, −₹2.36L, 5 red days); by month Jul +₹2.26L, Aug +₹3.24L, Sep +₹1.92L, Oct +₹2.33L (V5: +4.19L, −0.41L, +3.47L, +3.50L). Less in total, a smaller worst day and drawdown, every month positive. Caveats: the 150-point floor was picked in-sample (one of four values tried on the same data); 2.5% and 3.5% are the two targets Pramanaa and this platform already ran, not values fitted here; there is no SENSEX tick data, so exits run on one-minute bars and recorded snapshots. Sessions from 20 Oct are the first unseen ones. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.
