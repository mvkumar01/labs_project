Far-OTM expiry strangle · NIFTY + SENSEX (paper)

Every NIFTY weekly expiry (Tuesday) and SENSEX weekly expiry (Thursday), on the day the contract really expires. At 15:25 on the session before it, sell 1 lot of the call and 1 lot of the put of that contract: call nearest spot × (1 + 2σ), put nearest spot × (1 − 2σ), σ = the standard deviation of the index's last 20 daily close-to-close log returns. Sold at the bid, bought back at the ask at 15:25 on expiry day (a leg with no ask settles at intrinsic on the official close). Tail rule: from 09:15 on expiry day, if the pair's ask reaches 3× the entry credit, both legs are bought back at once; an overnight gap is not protected. Lots {{ st.lots.NIFTY if st.lots else 65 }} (NIFTY) and {{ st.lots.SENSEX if st.lots else 20 }} (SENSEX); short-option charges; margin about 10% of the index notional.

The style of the most consistent sellers: sell far out-of-the-money options into every expiry. Research (V2 Strategy Tester): NSE daily files 2019–2026, 806 expiries on four indices, +2.7 bps of notional an event, 96% up; on bid/ask April–September 2026, NIFTY +3.3 bps an event, SENSEX about zero. The price is the tail: one bad expiry cost 3–6% of notional in 2020. Shadow books: k = 3σ with the tail rule, and k = 2σ with no tail rule; each also at mid prices. Research period: entries before {{ st.forward_from or '2026-10-19' }}; forward after. Retirement, stated before the forward period: retire if one forward event loses more than 1.5% of the strangle's underlying notional, or if the first 26 forward events net to zero or less after charges. Quotes with bid and ask exist on this server from 1 June 2026, so the book starts with the expiries after it.
