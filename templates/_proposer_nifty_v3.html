{# Paper book: Nifty Scalper V3 #}

Nifty Scalper V3 (paper)

Nifty Scalper V1 with the predictor printing every 3 minutes instead of 5 — the paper copy of the live preset Nifty Scalper V3. First print once seven completed one-minute SENSEX bars exist (09:22), then one every 3 minutes. Everything else is Nifty Scalper V1: the Sensex V5 entry gate with the 75-point SENSEX with-the-day filter, NIFTY options (next weekly on NIFTY's expiry day, |delta| ≥ 0.60, ₹12L of premium per entry), scaled spot targets and 150-point floor, no premium floor, a 270 s wait after an exit and a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_nifty_v3_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.015) * 100) }}% day target.

Chosen on 27 Oct 2026: on that day's ticks the 3-minute prints made +₹936 at 3 lots against −₹11,710 on 5-minute prints; on the one-minute simulator, 1–26 Oct, 5/4/3/2-minute prints were within about ₹15k of each other (alphaIMB print_gap_test.py). Caveats: one day decides it; the history here is the one-minute simulator (no tick data before 27 Oct), which misses fills decided within seconds. The live preset trades on ticks. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.
