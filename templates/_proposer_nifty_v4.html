{# Paper book: Nifty Scalper V4 #}

Nifty Scalper V4 (paper)

Nifty Scalper V3 with two entry blocks — the paper copy of the live preset Nifty Scalper V4. A fresh entry signal is held when a NIFTY CPR level (S3–R3 with BC, P and TC, from the previous session) sits within 15 points on the side the trade must travel, or when the last finished one-minute NIFTY candle is a doji, hammer or inverted hammer. A held signal waits for the next print. Everything else is Nifty Scalper V3: prints every 3 minutes from 09:22, the Sensex V5 entry gate with the 75-point SENSEX with-the-day filter, NIFTY options (next weekly on NIFTY's expiry day, |delta| ≥ 0.60, ₹12L of premium per entry), scaled spot targets and 150-point floor, a 270 s wait after an exit and a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_nifty_v4_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.015) * 100) }}% day target, banked whatever the running book. A trade is closed early once NIFTY is 35 points against within 10 minutes of the entry (on the live preset from 9 Nov 2026; this history is replayed with it from 1 July).

Research, 1 Jul – 30 Oct 2026 on the one-minute simulator: 88 trades, +₹10.90L against V3's +₹1.29L (alphaIMB support_exit.py, REBUILD sec. 56–57). Caveats: in-sample; three days (10 Aug, 11 Sep, 25 Sep) carry most of the gain; the same blocks do not help Nifty Scalper V1 or Sensex V5. The history here is the one-minute simulator, which misses fills decided within seconds; the live preset trades on ticks. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API. The 35-point / 10-minute early exit, 1 Jul – 5 Nov: +₹11.40L against +₹11.46L without it, and +₹11.39L against +₹8.26L when each open minute meets its adverse extreme first; worst day −₹8k in both; on the 27 Oct – 5 Nov ticks it never fired (alphaIMB pessimistic_replay.py).
