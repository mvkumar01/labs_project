Nifty Omega (paper)

Forward paper starts 12 November 2026; rules refined 16 November 2026. NIFTY weekly options, two lots. A fresh five-minute alpha crossing of +30 or −30 requires EMA9 and EMA21 to agree on the completed candle. It is skipped when spot is within 10 points of the day's high (CALL) or low (PUT) as it stood 45 minutes earlier, or through it by less than 10; a later crossing can still trade. No entry on a Friday from 12:00. The strike is ATM, or on 0–1 days to expiry the strike with delta nearest 0.70 (up to 300 points in the money). One lot is sold when the bid reaches the entry ask +12.5, the other at +20. Both lots exit when alpha reaches +100 in favour, at −30 against a CALL or +20 against a PUT, and at the end of the day. After a PUT loss exit, one PUT re-entry is allowed when alpha crosses below 0 with the EMAs bearish, by 15:15.

Rule change from 23 November 2026 (snap exit): in the 2nd to 5th minute after the signal mark, if no target or alpha exit has fired, both lots are sold at the next minute's bid when the one-minute alpha has fallen back below +15 in the trade's direction and at least 60% of the in-range open-interest change since the signal is against the trade (exit reason snap_oi). The first snap exit of the day does not arm the PUT re-entry; instead one fresh entry is allowed on the next normal signal, either side, which then runs the full rules. Sessions before 23 November ran without it.
