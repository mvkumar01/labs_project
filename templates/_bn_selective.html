Bank Nifty Selective

BANKNIFTY monthly ATM options, one lot of 30 units. The strike range is the previous official close rounded to 100, plus/minus 400. Alpha is checked at completed five-minute marks.

Entry: a fresh crossing of +30 must already land at +60 or higher for a CALL; a fresh crossing of -30 must already land at -60 or lower for a PUT. Values beyond absolute alpha 200 are skipped. A later continuation to 60 does not qualify without a new fresh crossing.

Risk: maximum one trade per day, so a losing trade ends the session. Original exits apply: spot trail arms at +100 and trails by 50; alpha target at 100 in the trade's direction, alpha stop at -30 against it; otherwise close at 15:25. Entry ask, exit bid and charges determine option net P&L.

Dates before 11 November 2026 are historical research replays. Forward paper tracking starts from that date. This is a separate simulated book.
