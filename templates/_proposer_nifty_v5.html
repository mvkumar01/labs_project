Nifty Scalper V1 (paper)

The signals of the Sensex V5 2.5% book, unchanged — SENSEX prints every 300 s, the Proposer v3 entry gate with the 75-point SENSEX with-the-day filter, the opposite-print exit, a 270 s wait after an exit — traded on NIFTY options with NIFTY's own exits: the nearest NIFTY weekly (on NIFTY's own expiry day the next weekly), the shallowest ITM strike at ATM −/+50–150 with |delta| ≥ 0.60, ₹12L of premium per entry; spot targets 1.5× the SENSEX 100/40 scaled by NIFTY/SENSEX (about 46/19 points), the 150-point floor scaled (about 46 points), no premium floor, and a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_nifty_v5_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.015) * 100) }}% day target.

Picked on 1 Jul – 16 Oct as the best green-day rate of 27 NIFTY variants: 69 of 72 traded days green, +₹6.29L after charges, worst day −₹1.86L, max drawdown ₹3.53L (the best net of them was +₹8.45L at 93% green days with a 2.5% target). The Sensex V5 2.5% book on the same signals and period: +₹9.76L, worst day −₹1.97L. Caveats: in-sample (the variant was chosen on the same sessions); no NIFTY tick data, so prices inside a minute are the recorded snapshot plus a fitted delta; on some days (13 Oct) a 1.5% day-target exit, after the spread, falls short of the engine's 90% confirmation, so the book keeps trading and re-enters several times at about zero. Sessions from 22 Oct are the first unseen ones. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.
