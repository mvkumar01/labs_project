Nifty Scalper V4 2.5% (paper)

Nifty Scalper V4 with a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_nifty_v4_dt25_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.025) * 100) }}% day target instead of 1.5% — the paper copy of the live preset Nifty Scalper V4 with 2.5% chosen on the configure page. The day banks once today's realized profit plus the open trade reaches that share of the trade's entry capital, whatever the running book (no recovery mode), and a trade is closed early once NIFTY is 25 points against within 3 minutes of the entry (from 6 Nov 2026 on the live preset; this history is replayed with it from 1 July). Everything else is Nifty Scalper V4: entries held with a NIFTY CPR level within 15 points on the way or after a doji, hammer or inverted hammer one-minute candle, prints every 3 minutes from 09:22, NIFTY options (|delta| ≥ 0.60, ₹12L of premium per entry), scaled spot targets and 150-point floor, a 270 s wait after an exit.

Research, 1 Jul – 30 Oct 2026 on the one-minute simulator, banking whatever the book: 1.5% +₹10.90L (worst day −₹0.21L), 2% +₹10.37L (−₹3.13L), 2.5% +₹14.82L (97 trades, worst day −₹3.13L), 3% +₹8.65L (−₹5.89L) (alphaIMB v4_tail_risk.py round 4). Caveats: in-sample, and the four targets do not rise or fall in order; the larger target turns some banked days into floor exits. The history here is the one-minute simulator; the live preset trades on ticks. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API. With the 25-point / 3-minute early exit, the same replay through 5 Nov: +₹19.29L against +₹15.85L without it, worst day −₹1.73L against −₹3.13L, 9 early exits; almost all of the gain is 7 Oct, and on the 27 Oct – 5 Nov ticks it never fired (alphaIMB v4_early_exit.py).
