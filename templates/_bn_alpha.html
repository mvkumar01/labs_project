BANKNIFTY Alpha (paper)

Range = the previous session's official NIFTY BANK close rounded to 100, ±{{ st.half_width or 400 }}, locked before the open. Alpha = the standard alphaIMB formula on the nearest monthly expiry against the previous session's settled OI, read at every 5-minute mark. CALL when alpha crosses above +30, PUT when it crosses below −30; re-entry on the next fresh cross. Exits: a spot trail that arms at +100 points and trails by 50 (1-minute bars), alpha ±100 target, alpha −30 stop for calls (+30 for puts), otherwise 15:25. Paper leg: the ATM strike of the same monthly expiry, {{ st.lots or 1 }} lot of {{ st.lot_size or 30 }}, bought at the ask and sold at the bid, NSE charges; spot points are logged beside it.

Evidence status: in-sample, in spot points, no costs — 115 sessions (3 Mar – 21 Oct 2026): +7,368 points, 63.5% of days profitable, 126 trades, worst day −383. The ±400 width and the stop settings were chosen on those days. This book reproduces the research trade for trade on every session the labs store holds (from June). The option leg was not part of the research. Sessions from {{ st.first_unseen or '2026-10-26' }} are the first the rule was not chosen on. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.
