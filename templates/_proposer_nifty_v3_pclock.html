{# Paper book: Nifty Scalper V3 (Pramanaa timing) #}

Nifty Scalper V3 — Pramanaa timing (paper)

Nifty Scalper V3 on Pramanaa's print timing — paper only. Pramanaa's predictor counts the still-forming one-minute candle as the seventh bar, so its first call comes in the 09:21 minute (here 09:21:45, the median of its bot's first entries), and its loop prints every 310.5 s, so the minute slides through the day. Everything else is Nifty Scalper V1, with a {{ "%.1f"|format((proposer_nifty_v3_pclock_stats.daily_target_pct or 0.015) * 100) }}% day target.

On 27 Oct 2026 our predictor at Pramanaa's times gave the same call as Pramanaa at 14 of 15 prints; on ticks Nifty Scalper V1 on those prints made +₹1,121 at 3 lots with our gap regime, −₹5,372 with Pramanaa's neutral one. Caveats: Pramanaa's real timing drifts a few seconds a day, so this lands on its minute most days, not every day; on one-minute data the forming candle is priced at the last completed close. Paper only: it never calls a broker order API.
