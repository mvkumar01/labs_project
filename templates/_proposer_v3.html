SENSEX Proposer v3 (paper)

The live SENSEX Proposer's rules, replayed on paper at {{ proposer_v3_stats.lots or 100 }} lots, with one entry filter: only with the day. A trade is taken only when SENSEX's last completed 1-minute close is at least {{ "%.0f"|format(proposer_v3_stats.with_day_pts or 75) }} points beyond the 09:15 close in the trade's direction — calls when the day is up, puts when it is down. Everything else is the current live bot: gap-rule regime read at the 09:15 close, 5-class print every five minutes, nearest weekly ATM ∓200 ITM, +100 / +40 spot points, opposite print above 0.45, 2.5% day target, 15:25 — with the premium floor at −20% (the other presets stop at −30%). No Renko exit and no loss cap. The companion 3.5% book changes only the day target and has the same full-history start.

Day-target evidence: in the 1 June–8 October Labs replay at 100 lots, 3.5% averaged +₹8.4 lakh across the 20 / 25 / 30-second print phases versus +₹6.1 lakh at 2.5%, but its bad-phase drawdowns were deeper. In Pramanaa's forward paper bots from 3 September–14 October at 25 lots, 3.5% made ₹2,09,428 after charges versus ₹1,50,556 at 2.5%, with the same 40 trades and 35 winners. This is a fit on history plus six weeks of forward paper, not a guarantee; the operator intends to start at one lot.
Evidence status: a fit. The 75-point level was chosen on the sessions from 1 June to 8 October 2026; on those it cut the trades ending worse than ₹1 lakh from about 33 to 8 and the worst day from about −₹10 lakh to −₹2.4 lakh, with most of the extra profit coming from June. The −20% floor was added on 10 October from the same sessions: none of v3's winning trades there dipped below −20% before winning, and most of its sizeable losers passed it on the way to −30%. Sessions from {{ proposer_v3_stats.first_unseen or '2026-10-09' }} are the first it was not chosen on. Fills inside a minute are estimates (recorded quote at the minute plus a fitted delta times the SENSEX move along the bar's open → extreme → extreme → close path, half the recorded spread each way, then charges), and totals move by lakhs with the second of the minute the entry lands on.
