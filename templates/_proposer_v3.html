SENSEX Proposer v3 (paper)

The live SENSEX Proposer's rules, replayed on paper at {{ proposer_v3_stats.lots or 100 }} lots, with one entry filter: only with the day. A trade is taken only when SENSEX's last completed 1-minute close is at least {{ "%.0f"|format(proposer_v3_stats.with_day_pts or 75) }} points beyond the 09:15 close in the trade's direction — calls when the day is up, puts when it is down. Everything else is the current live bot: gap-rule regime read at the 09:15 close, 5-class print every five minutes, nearest weekly ATM ∓200 ITM, −30% premium floor, +100 / +40 spot points, opposite print above 0.45, 2.5% day target, 15:25. No Renko exit and no loss cap.

Evidence status: a fit. The 75-point level was chosen on the sessions from 1 June to 8 October 2026; on those it cut the trades ending worse than ₹1 lakh from about 33 to 8 and the worst day from about −₹10 lakh to −₹2.4 lakh, with most of the extra profit coming from June. Sessions from {{ proposer_v3_stats.first_unseen or '2026-10-09' }} are the first it was not chosen on. Fills inside a minute are estimates (recorded quote at the minute plus a fitted delta times the SENSEX move along the bar's open → extreme → extreme → close path, half the recorded spread each way, then charges), and totals move by lakhs with the second of the minute the entry lands on.
